When a driver crosses the stop line and stops while the traffic light in the direction of travel is red, what is the penalty classification?

  1. 1. Violation of intersection passage method
  2. 2. Failure to come to a temporary stop
  3. 3. Traffic signal violation
  4. 4. Failure to drive slowly
Reveal Official Answer & Explanation
✅ Official Answer: #3

If you stop beyond the stop line when the traffic light is red, you are punished for a signal violation.

🇰🇷 고속도로 운전 중 교통사고 발생 현장에서의 운전자 대응방법으로 바르지 않는 것은?

Which of the following is an INCORRECT response by a driver at the scene of a traffic accident while driving on an expressway?

  1. 1. Administer first aid depending on the degree of injury of the passengers.
  2. 2. Inform following drivers of the danger, such as by turning on the hazard warning lights.
  3. 3. Direct traffic behind the accident vehicle until a police officer arrives.
  4. 4. Move to a safe place to prevent a secondary accident.
Reveal Official Answer & Explanation
✅ Official Answer: #3

The rear of an accident vehicle carries a risk of a secondary accident, so it is desirable to move to a safe place.

What is the penalty standard if a driver knowingly uses counterfeit petroleum products (fake fuel) as vehicle fuel?

  1. 1. Administrative fine of 50,000 won to 100,000 won
  2. 2. Administrative fine of 500,000 won to 1 million won
  3. 3. Administrative fine of 2 million won to 20 million won
  4. 4. Not punishable.
Reveal Official Answer & Explanation
✅ Official Answer: #3

Petroleum and Alternative Fuel Business Act Enforcement Decree, Attached Table 6, Standards for Imposing Administrative Fines: If a person knowingly uses fake petroleum products as vehicle fuel, an administrative fine of from 2 million won to 20 million won may be imposed depending on the amount used.
